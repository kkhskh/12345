# Final language audit

## Passes

- Connected paragraphs in the main manuscript; methods use short labeled blocks only where intervention families must be distinguished.
- No “groundbreaking,” “paves the way,” “hidden causal substrate,” “we are the first.”
- “Causal” is tied to interventions and to “causal summary/description,” not to sufficiency except where sufficiency is denied.
- Official negatives named in prose.

## Quoted weak sentences and corrections already applied

**Draft instinct:** “The learned representation exhibited compelling bidirectional generalization with residual limitations.”

**Used instead:** “On a fresh 32-triplet cohort, mean target advantages were positive, but joint switching of both arms occurred in 13 of 32 triplets, below a 60% gate.”

**Draft instinct:** “Equal output JS means interventions were equally strong.”

**Used instead:** “Mean residual L2 after calibration was 5.30 (correct), 6.37 (wrong), and 12.27 (random).”

**Draft instinct:** “Donor-free transfer recovers the target state.”

**Used instead:** “Mean \(Y_{\mathrm{learned}}\) was \(-0.582\)\ldots the absolute score stayed negative.”

**Remaining roughness (not silently polished into a new claim):**

1. Introduction still says “the complementary experiment is less standard.” That is a literature judgment, not a priority claim. Acceptable; do not upgrade to “first.”
2. “Recorded” appears often. That repetition is intentional provenance, not a style flourish. After Level 1, many instances can become “we measured.”
3. Section 3 uses labeled intervention blocks. They are lists of methods, not a results dump. Keep.
4. The sentence “Nothing here is evidence about large language models…” is blunt. Keep. Do not replace with “opens avenues for future work.”

## Tense and terminology

Intervention descriptions are present tense for definitions, past tense for executed studies. “Probe-invisible,” “probe-preserving,” and “kernel” are used as synonyms after definition; prefer “probe-preserving” in captions. \(Y\) is never called accuracy.

## Passive voice

Methods still use “was frozen,” “were generated,” which is appropriate for protocol. Results lead with the measurement, not “It was found that.”
