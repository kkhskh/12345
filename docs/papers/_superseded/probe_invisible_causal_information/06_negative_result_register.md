# Negative result register

Official letters are preserved. None of the following is rewritten as a success.

## 1. 08R2 validation — `08R2_VALIDATION_COMPLETE — NEGATIVE`

Editor A, a full-space residual editor with a board-decoding objective and L2 budget 16, did not establish behavioral repair on the frozen 08R2 validation pairs. Exact numerical pair tables are **NOT AVAILABLE FROM ARCHIVED ARTIFACTS** in this workspace. The letter is the scientific fact that remains.

This negative result is part of the empirical boundary: a probe-targeted edit that tries to install a decoded board is not, by itself, a working legal-move intervention in this protocol.

## 2. Original COST equal-JS — `TARGET_SPECIFIC_AT_EQUAL_JS_INCONCLUSIVE`

The first attempt to match output Jensen–Shannon divergence did not yield a supported equal-JS specificity letter. A later forensic recorded `FROZEN_COST_PROTOCOL_DEVIATION_CONFIRMED`: calibration used a donor-reference procedure rather than independently matching each of the seven directions to JS = 0.01.

The restored fixed-JS study is a separate, protocol-compliant execution. The original COST numbers must not be presented as that execution.

## 3. Leftover symmetric-target census — `SYMMETRIC_TARGET_CENSUS_INFEASIBLE`

After NST consumed leftover B-category games, the unused-validation pool contained 0 B triplets under the frozen 16A+16B design. This is a sampling constraint, not a software failure and not a causal null.

## 4. Fresh symmetric switching — `SYMMETRIC_TARGET_SWITCHING_NOT_SUPPORTED`

On 32 fresh triplets, natural-anchor FULL had positive mean \(A_T\) and positive mean \(A_W\). Joint both-positive switching occurred in 13/32 triplets (40.6%), below the prespecified 60% gate. Coverage was 32/32. Stage 6 recomputed 768/768 saved Y values in the Colab run.

The official conclusion is that **reliable simultaneous switching was not supported**. Positive average target discrimination is not the same claim.

## 5. Visible/nullspace factorial — `TARGET_SPECIFIC_VISIBLE_NULL_INTERACTION_NOT_SUPPORTED`

On 32 independently seeded triplets, with \(\rho=\nu=3.5\) and equal total L2,

\[
I_T = 0.000808,\quad \mathrm{CI}\ [-0.000940,\ 0.003017]
\]

\[
I_W = 0.000972,\quad \mathrm{CI}\ [-0.000086,\ 0.002208].
\]

Both missed the +0.005 floor. Both intervals include 0. Coverage 32/32. Stage 6 saved 128/128 factorial logits.

This does not prove that no interaction exists under any intervention. It shows that this controlled factorial hypothesis failed. The map was not retrained in response.

## 6. STATE-SEMANTICS increments below the +0.01 floor

FULL minus LEGAL_ONLY and FULL minus BOARD_ONLY were recorded below the prespecified meaningful-effect floor. The exploratory letter is `LEGAL_MASK_SUFFICES_IN_TESTED_FAMILY`, not a claim that the legal mask is uniquely necessary.

## 7. Absolute target-response scores

NST mean \(Y_{\mathrm{learned}} \approx -0.582\) versus \(Y_{\mathrm{EditorA}} \approx -0.636\). The learned map improved a still-negative score. It did not make the model behave as if it had adopted the target board.

## 8. Nonexact-board weakness (session record)

Session scientific notes recorded that nonexact target-board subgroups had confidence intervals versus wrong/random that included zero. Pair-level tables are not local. The qualitative limitation is retained: the transfer is weaker when the frozen probe does not already decode the target board.

## Inclusion rule for the manuscript

A negative result enters the main text if it constrains the interpretation of probe-invisible target transfer (08R2 validation, COST protocol split, switching, factorial, absolute Y, stage share). Failed leftover census is mentioned once as a design infeasibility, not as a causal finding. Unrelated IOI or factual-conflict pilots are not part of the Othello sample and are not used as evidence.
