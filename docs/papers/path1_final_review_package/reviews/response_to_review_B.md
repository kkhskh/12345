# Response to Review B

Review B is `reviews/review_B_statistics_causal.md`. The recomputed headline numbers in that review match the manuscript. The changes below are the ones a saved file could support. No study was rerun, and the sealed confirmation split was not opened.

## Changed

The matched-divergence section no longer calls the first run a deviation from the fixed-0.01 protocol. That run followed the protocol that was committed first: controls matched to the correct direction's own divergence, with a floor of 0.05, and the letter is inconclusive because the matching was incomplete. The fixed-0.01 protocol, with its floor of 0.02, was written afterward on the same 48 pairs. The follow-up meets the later floor. It does not replace the inconclusive letter, and it is not scored against the earlier floor, because that floor applied at a different divergence. The phrase "preregistered before outcomes" is no longer applied to the 0.02 floor. Secondary targets 0.005 and 0.02 are named as not run.

The 56% figure is still the ratio of means against Editor A, and the text now says that this is not evidence of a stage-specific correction. A random kernel direction already accounts for part of the gain, as added after Review A.

Every category-B pair is also an exact-decode pair, in the matched-divergence study and in NST, so those two slices are not separate evidence. The pair-level correct-minus-wrong contrasts in the two studies correlate 0.155.

After subtracting the Editor A score, the learned score correlates 0.846 with the oracle and 0.452 with the wrong-target score. The raw correlation near 0.998 is still described as a baseline effect.

In SPEC, 31 of the 48 pairs share a wrong-donor game with another pair. The largest linked group has 7 pairs, not one group of 40. In FIBER, one target game is used in 25 of the 32 pairs. Same-seed interval agreement within \(3\times10^{-8}\) is labeled as seed agreement, not a Monte Carlo bound. The selected \(k\) is the top of its grid, and cross-validated \(R^2\) was still rising there. The abstract says the positive results reuse validation pairs and that the two later cohorts are the only fresh samples.

## Not changed, and why

The result is not described as failing the 0.05 floor. That comparison mixes two estimands. A rerun on the sealed confirmation split was not authorized. SEM, FRESH, and VNF pair arrays are still missing, so the stage share still has no interval, and VNF main effects are still unreported. The four-decimal table was left as is. The two-route stage identity is still shown, and it is still only a check that the two archival paths agree.
