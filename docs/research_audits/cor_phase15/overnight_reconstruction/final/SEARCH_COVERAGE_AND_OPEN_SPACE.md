# Search coverage and open space

## Covered, with a theorem or a counterexample

- Constant versus centered decomposition of a single-token edit, as a cited lemma, rechecked on seed 7.
- Finite versus infinitesimal level set of LayerNorm, including one zero gain and two zero gains.
- Weight-free budget B0 on non-constant edits.
- Lipschitz certificates against Cauchy–Schwarz, plus one explicit majorant.
- Factor-kernel exactness, its failure under Jacobian membership, and its collapse when `QKV` is injective.
- Manufactured non-constant point: scales, a 400-edit sample, gradient inner product versus `R`.
- Exotic-zero search on seed 0 at five radii. Minima positive. Not a proof of absence.
- `O`/`D` algebra, metric change, opposite readouts, zero cochain.
- Deletion necessity versus interaction.
- Value-only sign, remainder with `Dm=0`, feature-3 fiber, final LN versus raw readout.
- Structural residual zero on recomputed traces with both margin signs.
- One residual norm, two sources.

## Named and not searched

Stacks, post-norm, parallel residuals, RMSNorm in place of LayerNorm, rotary scores, multi-query attention, trained operating points, and rates over width or depth. A rate would not turn an existence counterexample into a mechanism. A post-norm shift is a different calculation and was not claimed.

## Open inside the block

Whether some non-constant `δ` at a Gaussian weight makes the full branch increment exactly zero without `ΔX` lying in the factor kernel. The search did not find one. Local non-vanishing holds along directions where the branch linear form is nonzero, because the map is analytic and the first derivative is then nonzero. That still allows isolated large-amplitude zeros. Those zeros, if any, would be defined by running the branch.

Whether a state-dependent majorant, using the minimum of `v` along the segment and still not using `Dm`, can be written with a constant below the explicit `Λ` and above `||g||`. Theorem L forbids the part below `||g||`. The intermediate improvement was not needed and was not claimed as a predictor.

## Not open

A weight-free centered bound. A Lipschitz certificate that beats Cauchy–Schwarz. A COR reading of `O` or `D`. A cohomological constraint. Phase 2.
