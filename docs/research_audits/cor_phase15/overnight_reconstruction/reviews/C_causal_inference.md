# Review C. Causal inference

Same cloud environment. Not an external referee.

**Objection.** Theorem K builds the weights after seeing `δ`, so the intervention family was selected to fit the prediction.

**Resolution.** The existence statement is conditional and constructed. It shows the conditional sentence is not vacuous. It does not show that a fixed Gaussian block has a non-constant exact family. On seeds 0–11 the `QKV` stack is injective, and Theorem F then leaves only constants. The prediction, once the weights are fixed, is checked by recomputing `R` without putting `R` into the kernel products. That check passes on the constructed point and is not a discovery procedure for natural weights.

**Objection.** `g·δ = 0.10665` while `R=0` might be a clamp rather than the intervention `do`.

**Resolution.** The gradient is the derivative of the same recomputed block. The branch tensor at the designed `δ` is zero to `10^{-15}`, so the finite map really is the skip. The derivative sees a different, infinitesimal direction, which Theorem J separates on another weight setting. Both facts are about `do`, not about a value-only clamp.

**Status.** The causal reading of Theorem K as a general response law is unsupported. The equality under the kernel hypothesis stands.
