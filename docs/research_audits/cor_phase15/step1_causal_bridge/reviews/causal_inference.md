# Reviewer B — causal inference

Independent pass. The reviewer read files 02, 04, 05, and 07. The objections below are the reviewer's.

## Objections

1. **Theorem NFL's first proof never built a structural downstream map.** It differentiated a functional of an assigned cochain. File 04's response clamps one site and recomputes. On the identity edge at `(0,0)`, with the logit on the child, the frozen-cochain derivative of a parent displacement is `0` and the recomputed response is `+1`.

2. **"Recompute descendants" is order-dependent when one intervention target is downstream of the other.** Hard intervention needs an explicit truncation rule. Overlap of descendant sets is not the same as nesting. Two incomparable parents of one child have an unambiguous joint clamp. Family H's original proviso did not say this, and a four-term contrast is not the mixed Hessian of one untruncated map.

3. **CE-19 and CE-20 do not isolate recomputation from stitching**, because they also move the logit. A stitch whose logit reads the assigned child has a large output effect. A recomputed exact map whose logit reads the child has residual `0` and a nonzero effect. Residual and response come apart in both directions. The operational distinction in file 04 is still the right one.

4. **Descent on `D` is not a file-04 intervention under exact commutation.** Theorem Z puts every legal one-site recomputed trajectory on `ker d`. The `M`-normal direction moves parent and child independently.

5. **Residual addition does not imply `I=0`.** For `v=u+w` and `y=v^2`, `I=2` while the aggregation residual is `0` on all four recomputed worlds. A measured `I` is an estimand. Using it to predict its own joint term is the circular misuse. The contrast itself is not thereby illegitimate.

6. **File 07 collapsed three different facts:** `R_c` is not a function of `D`; the score depends on conventions; some fits use the outcome. A fixed network and a fixed `do` identify `R_c` by recomputation. A train-only high-capacity probe does not automatically encode evaluation labels. The repair objective's gradient uses `∇m`; the scalar `O` is not itself an attribution mixture. Sutter et al. concern interchange-accuracy classes, not automatically `||ρ s_u - s_v||` on observational activations.

The reviewer judged that no map from `(P,ρ,M,W)` or from `D` to the sign of a recomputed response survives. The wording that does not survive is the claim that the first NFL writeup already covered file-04 responses, and the claim that Family H's formula is a mixed Hessian whenever descendants overlap.
