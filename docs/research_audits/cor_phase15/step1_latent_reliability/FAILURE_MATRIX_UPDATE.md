# Update to COR failures F1–F7, F25, F29, F30 (this package only)

**Historical verdicts are unchanged.** Each row rates only the new evidence from this package.

**Evidence labels.**
- **[R]**: frozen or registered result on the confirmation split.
- **[PH]**: post hoc, exploratory.

| id | failure | new evidence | update |
|---|---|---|---|
| F1 | missing noncircular causal bridge | **First positive learned bridge in the COR program, scoped.** A relation that is independently specified (within-group agreement across the *other* questions), and that is Bayes-sufficient for the latent reliability (Theorem S), changes candidate decisions through an identified computation: last-layer reliability-gated attention routing. Evidence: [R] Type B 3/3 T1/M seeds; pattern patch IIA 0.84–0.85, KL recovered 0.99. The relation is not the target: on `target` pairs the routing does not transfer the answer, and the tag's content is reliability, not values (R² ≤ 0.007 for own reports). Where it is used non-monotonically (T4) the same circuit implements the non-monotone Bayes map [PH, curriculum]. | **positive progress, scoped.** For T1 a bridge exists at the routing level (registered); tag localisation is seed-dependent. The T4 bridge is post hoc and needs a T1 curriculum |
| F2 | lower discrepancy can worsen a candidate decision | T4 demonstrates the failure at the Bayes level: the most coherent group can be the colluder. Learned T1 models apply "agreement ⇒ reliable" to T4 data and drop to 0.36 (review B5). T4-curriculum models reverse this for perfectly agreeing groups (the tag drops at leave-one-out count 5) [PH]. | **sharpened**: whether more agreement helps is task-dependent, and a learned model can encode the non-monotone map |
| F3 | consistency ranking does not determine candidate preference | In T4 the ranking by agreement does not give the ranking by reliability (non-monotone at full agreement). Preference follows the reliability posterior, not the consistency rank. | unchanged in substance (sharpened negative for "consistency rank ⇒ preference") |
| F4 | no distinctive prospective prediction | Prediction made before confirmation: the routing heads' group attention tracks P(group reliable), log-linear in counts for T1 and non-monotone for T4. Confirmed on T1 [R] (head R² 0.85–0.99) and on T4-curriculum s0 [PH]. The prediction follows from Bayes + attention gating; it is not unique to a "consistency theory". | **partial**: a falsifiable prediction was confirmed, but it is the Bayes/truth-discovery prediction |
| F5 | representation and map non-identifiability | The same function is implemented with different localisations: the key-side tag (s0, s1) or a key/query split (s2). The tag encodes a group-indexed contrast of all counts in a seed-dependent coordinate system. "Consistency score" and "group statistic" cannot be separated in this generator (review B3). | **confirmed limit**: the routing is identifiable; the representational localisation is not unique |
| F6 | output optimization hidden in repair objectives | Mediator candidates are defined from Bayes targets (D1) or input-level contrast pairs (D2). No output-trained subspace is used. | **progress** (procedural) |
| F7 | outcome-dependent tuning | Freeze before main training. Two addenda were registered after pilot dev data only. Every post-confirmation change is in DEVIATIONS.md and reported alongside the frozen verdicts. The frozen primary answer is unchanged by post hoc work. | **progress** (procedural) |
| F25 | same consistency, different decisions | T4: identical agreement counts for the reliable group and the colluder can give opposite routing, depending on the other groups' counts. Same consistency, different decision, and correctly so. | **sharpened** (now a demonstrated Bayes-correct case, and learned [PH]) |
| F29 | no demonstrated advantage over existing methods | The mechanism is ordinary: reliability estimation from agreement plus a reliability-weighted vote (Dawid–Skene / truth-discovery structure), implemented as attention gating. The identification method is difference-of-means contrast PCA plus interchange patching. | **unchanged** (no new-theory advantage) |
| F30 | excessive unfrozen choices | Thresholds were frozen. The review showed weak nulls: a mismatched-source IIA of about 0.45 and a target-change floor of about 0.05. The degenerate reuse criteria (P6, K6′) and the trivially satisfied "distributed" verdict were flagged and reported with gated versions. One registered T1 seed fails K4′ by 0.004. | **partial**: frozen, but several criteria were weaker than intended |
