# 1. EXACT SCIENTIFIC STARTING POINT

Read the original evidence from Investigations IX–XII, together with relevant earlier COR findings.

Investigation XII achieved genuine progress:

- Freshly pretrained Transformers replicated the layer-1 relational interaction.
- Causal interventions showed that altering the layer-1 contribution can change candidate decisions in the predicted direction.
- Different models implemented the interaction through heterogeneous attention and MLP pathways.
- Cross-model mapped interventions supported a transferable eligibility-related abstraction.
- The mathematically correct two-colluder posterior was derived for the actual shared-copying generator.
- A full downstream Transformer block could be retrained to solve the two-colluder task using frozen early-layer representations.
- Independent reviewing agents detected important errors, several of which were corrected.

However, the following limitations are scientifically consequential.

## 1.1 The decision-combination circuit remains unidentified

We have increasingly detailed evidence about the construction of eligibility-related information in layers 0–1.

But **layer 2, where this information affects candidate-specific evidence, has not received equivalent causal reconstruction**.

The existing evidence does not show exactly how the network implements the trained weighting rule, how evidence is routed to the candidate readout, or how different relational statistics are combined.

This is the leading unexplained mechanistic problem.

## 1.2 The six-dimensional subspace is not yet a uniquely identified natural mediator

The learned DAS subspace was optimized for counterfactual interchange.

Several scrubbing and restoration experiments therefore partly retest the objective used to construct that subspace.

Natural donor activations do not necessarily produce natural states after subspace replacement.

The known possibility of interpretability illusions from learned activation subspaces remains relevant.

Further, mean ablation of layer 1 leaves nontrivial eligibility-related decision behavior, and the complement of the six-dimensional subspace retains decision-relevant information.

We must determine whether this represents redundant information, an alternative mechanism, downstream recomputation, or a limitation of the intervention method.

## 1.3 The mechanistic description is not universal

Investigation XII falsified its prediction of universally redundant perfectness routing.

Complete confirmation parents were predominantly attention-pattern-dependent, but some incomplete parents were different, and P156 formed much of the interaction in MLP1.

Pattern/value attribution also depended on the reference activation.

Do not describe a particular pattern/value split or softmax computation as a universal COR mechanism.

The correct research question is what computation survives these implementation differences.

## 1.4 Some intervention metrics were weak or non-discriminating

Investigation XII's independent review established that:

- `path_inA` and `resamp_A` represented the same intervention.
- `path_inA` was poorly suited to distinguishing the conjunction hypothesis from simple information relay.
- The binary comparison of KL to Bayes versus KL to the ineligible-group counterfactual was biased by their different posterior entropies.
- P6's coded criterion could pass under a null intervention.
- The linear-versus-projection P6 test could also pass with a random subspace.
- High interchange accuracy was not sufficient to identify the correct variable.

These are not merely reporting concerns.

They affect what causal conclusions can legitimately be drawn.

## 1.5 The generalization interpretation remains incomplete

For two colluders, the earlier independent-copying Bayesian reference was wrong for the actual generator.

The corrected shared-copying model requires a joint between-group relation, denoted \(J_H\), that per-group eligibility alone cannot express.

Retraining a full downstream block recovered near-Bayesian decisions, even when the six-dimensional eligibility subspace was patched.

But that does **not** prove the repair avoids all eligibility information or that only the evidence-aggregation function changed.

The full block has substantial computational capacity, and the larger residual representation still contains information.

The repaired decision mechanism must be identified directly.

## 1.6 Fresh confirmation and protocol integrity require improvement

Four of seven new pretrained parents failed the readiness criterion.

The protocol amendment added parents after training had started, reversed a frozen restriction, and inaccurately described what training outputs existed at the time.

Although the main verdicts survived the restricted original-cohort analysis, this is not acceptable as a model for future prospective confirmation.

The next investigation must implement a stronger, auditable protocol.

# 2. CENTRAL RESEARCH QUESTION

**How do Transformers transform relational information into candidate-specific evidence, and what distinguishes an information representation that supports the correct decision from a learned algorithm that actually computes it?**

Investigate the following competing explanations.

**H1 — Shared relational evidence-combination algorithm**

Different models implement an equivalent candidate-evidence transformation despite heterogeneous neural pathways.

**H2 — Shared information, distinct downstream algorithms**

Models contain similar relational representations but transform them into candidate decisions differently.

**H3 — Redundant or distributed computation**

The apparent mediator is only one of several routes carrying decision-relevant information.

**H4 — Intervention-dependent explanation**

Some apparent mechanisms are consequences of learned alignment, synthetic activation construction, or evaluation geometry rather than uniquely identified natural computation.

**H5 — Source-model-dependent computation**

The correct relational operation depends on the structure of the information-generating process. No single eligibility variable or evidence-combination rule is sufficient across all source models.

These possibilities may coexist.

Design experiments and mathematics that can distinguish them.

## C4. Establish a mathematical boundary

# 15. FINAL DIRECTIVE — SCIENTIFIC DEPTH OVER CHECKLIST COMPLETION

Investigations IX–XII established increasingly strong evidence that independently trained Transformers can construct relational information, use it in candidate decisions, and exhibit common high-level computational structure despite heterogeneous neural implementations.

But Investigation XII also exposed a fundamental limitation.

**Constructing the correct relational information is not equivalent to computing the correct decision from that information.**

The downstream operation depends on the structure of the source-generating process.

Under changed collusion or copying relationships, the required statistics and computations may change.

The neural network may retain relevant information yet continue using an inappropriate decision algorithm.
