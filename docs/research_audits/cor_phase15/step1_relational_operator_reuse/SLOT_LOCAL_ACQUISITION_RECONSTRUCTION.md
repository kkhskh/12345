`src/xviislot.SlotDiag`, diagnostic stream 99_800_000, 1500 episodes.

## 5. How common it is

**XVI scan of pooled F_G** (16 acquisitions; `logs/expl` notes):
- Only C_s202 shows a long partial window (pooled F_G at intermediate values over 36 points, 1750 steps).
- All the others pass through intermediate pooled values within 1–9 diagnostic points (25–200 steps).

**Per-slot reconstruction of an ordinary arity-2 transition** (WB_low_s503, 10-step resolution):
- Slot 1 acquires first (F_G 0.19 at step 1540, 0.85 at 1700) and holds it alone for about 150 steps.
- Slot 3 follows at about 1780; slots 0, 2, 4 and 5 at 1820–1860.
- So even short transitions are **staggered across slots**. The long C_s202 window is an extreme of a general pattern.

## 6. Fresh-model reconstruction [frozen P0: MIXED]

- **Arity 3:** all 3 acquiring fresh models staggered. Slots acquired over 60–100 steps (corrected, W15), with partial checkpoints at 20-step resolution. A3_s801 never acquired the gate within 5000 steps.
- **Arity 2:** only 3 of 8 had a resolvable partial checkpoint. The other 5 acquired the gate in all slots within 20–40 steps.
- **No fresh model** reproduced a long partial window like C_s202's.
- **So:** the phenomenon of slot-local acquisition is reproducible at arity 3 in short form. Its long form is rare (1 of 16 XVI acquisitions, 0 of 11 fresh). At arity 2, in this condition, slots often acquire the gate together within the measurement resolution.
