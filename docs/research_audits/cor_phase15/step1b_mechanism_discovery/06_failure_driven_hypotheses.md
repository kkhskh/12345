# 06 — What the failures force

| Failure | Missing ingredient | Candidate structure | Status after this step |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lower `D` can worsen `L` | Sign | `∇L`, or a monotone assumption that is the conclusion | Sign lives in the readout. N1 |
| Exact compatibility, residual 0 | A quantity that still varies | `L` itself, or a projected leftover, or a stitch | Exact full-state residual cannot rank decisions |
| Approximate transport looks like conflict | A separator of surrogate error from interference | None found that uses only `(P,ρ,s)` | Unresolved as identification, not as a score |
| Probe-preserving edit changes output | Sufficiency of `P` | Kernel of `P` is causally live | Direction 1. Not COR |
| Two interventions interact | Shared nonlinearity or nested truncation | Hessian / truncated SCM | Established, not `D` |
| Attribution beats obstruction | Information in `∇L` that `D` discarded | N1: `D` is blind along some directions `L` uses | Explains the empirical gap without refitting it |

The category "consistency of local states" is not forced by these failures. What is forced is: any predictor of signed `R` must not be invariant in the directions `L` uses, and any predictor of `I` must know whether addends share a nonlinear map. Those are necessary conditions (file 21), not a repaired `O`.
