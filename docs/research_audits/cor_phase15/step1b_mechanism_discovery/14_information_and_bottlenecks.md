# 14 — Family H: information flow and minimal causal state

Mutual information between a site and a label does not prove causal use. Linear unreadability does not prove causal absence (Direction 1). Those two facts are the reason a probe nullspace can matter.

A collection of partial states can be jointly sufficient when none is (N2). The structure that admits a consistency relation is extra. In the additive example the consistency relation is independent of `L`. In a bottleneck `z = T(h)` that `L` actually factors through, the minimal causal state for residual-site edits that preserve `ker T` is `z` itself. Relations among coordinates of `z` matter only if `q` uses those relations.

**Hypothesis H-BOTT.** There exists a low-dimensional `z` such that a named intervention family is equivalent to intervening on `z`. This is again causal abstraction. Identification without evaluation leakage is the open field problem (DAS versus unrestricted maps). It is not solved here and is not an obstruction score.

**Status.** `REDUNDANT_WITH_EXISTING_THEORY` for the hypothesis; `UNIDENTIFIED` for a unique `z` in a real Transformer; `PROVED` for the distinction between decodability and use, by Direction 1 and Elazar et al. (2021), which this step does not re-run.
