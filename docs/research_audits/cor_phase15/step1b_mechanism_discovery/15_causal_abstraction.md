# 15 — Family I: abstractions that are not vacuous

Faithfulness: high-level and low-level models agree under a specified class of mechanism transformations (Geiger et al., 2025). Interchange-intervention accuracy is one graded score. Partial abstractions can be combined (Pîslar, Magliacane, Geiger, 2025). Unrestricted alignments can fit an arbitrary algorithm, including a randomly initialized network on IOI (Sutter, Minder, Hofmann, Pimentel, 2025).

Restrictions that make an alignment meaningful have to come from the frozen computation: a declared linear class, a declared site, a declared intervention family, frozen before seeing the evaluation score. Complexity penalties are not a uniqueness theorem.

Different valid abstractions can coexist: a circuit in one basis, a distributed subspace in another, both faithful on the same family and contradictory as stories about "the" variable. That is superposition / non-uniqueness (Update Proposition 7; Experiment 0B), not repaired here.

A COR coboundary is not interchange faithfulness. Zero linear residual is neither necessary nor sufficient for it (Step 1A, after Reviewer C). If the strongest route is causal abstraction, the honest classification is that it is causal abstraction.
