# 17 — Family K: repair without an obstruction objective

Start from `R_c`, not from `min D`.

A residual-site edit `δ` changes `L` by `w·δ` to first order, or by the finite recomputed increment. Target specificity relative to a probe is `Pδ=0` and `ΔL` large, which is kernel support of `L`. Preservation of unrelated behavior is a constraint on other readouts. Transfer is a statement about a map from symbolic state to `δ` (Direction 1's donor-free study). Failure under target switching is a statement about that map, already recorded as `SYMMETRIC_TARGET_SWITCHING_NOT_SUPPORTED`.

None of these conditions mentions `O`. A compatibility condition would have to emerge from the requirement that `δ` lie in a subspace that is independently the causal complement of a preserved feature. That is "edit in `ker P`" given a frozen `P`. It is a restriction on the intervention family, not an energy.

Importing `ΔL` into the objective and then crediting consistency is Update Proposition 1. Not used.

**Status.** Repair conditions are causal-response conditions. `PROVED_UNDER_RESTRICTED_ASSUMPTIONS` for the linear first-order case. No obstruction objective is necessary. Direction 1 remains independent and is not restated as COR.
