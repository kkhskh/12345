# 27 — Coverage

This is not a claim that every theory is impossible. It is the map of this search.

**Explored with formal refutation (scoped).** Unrestricted `D→L`; exact-intertwiner residual as a decision ranker; pairwise parent maps as the additive aggregator; pointwise-Jacobian stability formula; Euclidean `O` as intrinsic; `[ds]` as a cohomology class. Energy descent as "the block iterates on `D`" is rejected as an implementation claim for a standard feedforward block, not as a theorem that no implicit energy exists.

**Explored with conditional theorem (toys and linear algebra, not block theorems).** N1 (quantified over `L` or over kernel displacements); N2 existence; affine versus Jacobian; factorization for Lip bounds; two-node truncated `do`; `I=0` for affine `F` of two free arguments (an identity; the 50 draws are a check); `I=2` for one square; linear path inner products on frozen addends; majority vote on three bits; 3-d mean-std normalize. These are not Transformer-block theorems.

**Explored but unresolved.** A separator, using only `(P,ρ,s)`, of surrogate error from causal interference. Unique causal state of a real checkpoint. Whether any checkpoint implements a discrete correcting aggregator.

**Redundant with existing theory.** H-SUF, H-ABS, H-COORD, H-COMPOSE as named COR mechanisms, H-STAB.

**Promising but underidentified.** None as a COR score. Joint sufficiency and bottleneck abstractions are promising as causal-abstraction research and remain the field's identification problem.

**Not meaningfully explored.** Full multi-head attention with learned `W_Q`, `W_K`, `W_V` as a closed-form relational predictor; sequence-level LN across tokens; recurrent or test-time iteration; information-theoretic synergy measures as causal proofs; category-level reconstruction; any empirical Transformer measurement.

**Outside current scope.** Phase 2, Step 2 reconstruction, Direction 1 reruns, confirmation data, fitting maps to historical `Δm`.

A negative conclusion below is scoped to the explored cells. The unexplored empirical cells are why this is not "no future mechanism is possible."
