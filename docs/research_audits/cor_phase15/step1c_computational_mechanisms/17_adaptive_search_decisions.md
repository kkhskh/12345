# 17 — Adaptive search decisions

The search started from the four errors, because new mechanism language on top of a false kernel, a false metric cell, a false LayerNorm cell, or a false interaction sentence would have repeated Step 1B. Those four were settled before the block derivations were used as premises. The tests that match them passed as finite checks, and the proofs state the quantifiers the tests do not cover.

## What was deepened

Interaction was deepened because Step 1B's sentence was false and the true boundary is small: separability on a rectangle, not "nonlinear hence interacting." The boundary is checkable on the function that is actually intervened on. It stops the search from treating every MLP or every softmax as an interaction mechanism.

The attention split was deepened because the intervention semantics change the answer. Clamped `A` gives an exact theorem for `Y=AVW_O`. Residual edits of the bias-free map give an existence result and a zero-interaction boundary at `W_Q=0` with no bias and no LayerNorm. A query bias, and LayerNorm inside one token, are separate boundaries. None of these is the pre-norm residual `H+\mathrm{Attn}(\mathrm{LN}(H))` as a single theorem. The one-head formula already shows that a consistency residual is not an input to the value-linearity identity. It was not deepened into a multi-head stack.

The measurement kernel was deepened because N3's parenthetical was false and the repair is one operator. Further leftover taxonomies were not built. The operator-level statement already blocks the inference from "a projection was held fixed" to "the residual was held fixed."

LayerNorm was deepened far enough to separate computation, dual reparameterization, and plain RMSNorm. A full RMSNorm block was not derived. Othello-GPT is not an RMSNorm model, so the missing derivation does not affect the pre-norm claims. It does limit any sentence about other architectures. That limit is recorded in the coverage map rather than filled with a second theory.

## What was stopped

Majority vote, bottleneck abstraction under a new name, and further families of scalar `\rho` were not reopened. Step 1A and Step 1B already classified them, and the fiber theorem says a new scalar is informative only if its fibers match the response. Producing more scalars without that match does not raise the chance of a match.

No candidate was selected for a COR-style development. The selection rule in the assignment was survival under the objections that matter: independent identification, noncircularity, distinction from the Jacobian and from path patching, and a consequence for a named intervention. The value-linearity theorem survives as mathematics and fails the distinction test. It is the QK/OV decomposition. The fiber theorem survives and is the definition of sufficiency. The separability theorem survives and is calculus. None of them is a relational cause of a better decision.

The strongest negative result kept in view is N1 together with the recomputed-trace vacuity. Any later proposal that predicts sign from a statistic constant on a two-orientation family, or that predicts a response from a residual that is identically zero on the recomputed family, is already contradicted. That is the challenge a future restriction has to meet. It is not met by assuming the restriction "sign agrees with the score."

## Why these branches and not others

The branches that would change a conclusion are the ones that change a quantifier or an intervention row: identical versus pointwise interaction, `M` versus `W`, LN as computation versus a coordinate change, `ker P_u` versus `ker M_r`, `do(V)` versus `do(X)`. Those were the branches with a false or missing derivation behind them.

Multi-layer composition, trained operating points, and a search over learned probes would change an empirical conclusion only if a theorem said the extra layers create a new invariant. No such theorem was found at one layer. Running the model to look for one would be a new experiment. It was not authorized, and it is not a substitute for the missing one-layer implication.
