# Reviewer 5 — Reduction to existing theory

Provenance: subagent [reduction and coverage review](a33c4760-444a-4eff-aad9-53c2590e4ca6), this session, also covering Reviewers 7 and 8. Not an external referee.

Every positive statement that remained after the quantifier repairs is one of the following.

- Substitution into `I_F`, or differentiation and integration on a `C^2` rectangle. Calculus.
- Taylor's theorem for the infinitesimal interaction and for the affine expansion.
- Matrix multiplication `Y=AVW_O`. The QK/OV decomposition.
- The definition of a function on the fibers of `P`. Exact sufficiency. N1 is that definition on `\{\Delta,-\Delta\}` plus linearity of `L`.
- The definition of `\ker(P_v A-\rho P_u)`.
- The formula of `O` not containing `M`.
- `w·(h/\mathrm{rms})=(w·h)/\mathrm{rms}` for plain RMSNorm, and `w·LN(h)=(w·h)/\sigma` when `1^\top w=0`.
- Arithmetic of a sum `c_1+c_2`.

None of these is a relation, fixed without `R`, that implies a fact about `R` beyond the Jacobian, the Hessian, the path effect, or fiber constancy. `15` already said so. This pass did not find a surviving identity that escapes that list. The "iff" between zero interaction and `AVW_O` was the one place the draft claimed more than the reduction allows. It was removed.
