# 21 — What survives

Two kinds of statement survive. They are not the same kind.

## A proved identity, already the architecture

For every weight setting of this block and every `ε≥0`, a constant residual shift passes only through the skip, including when it is added on top of an arbitrary finite edit. `25` records that split. The finite response of the constant component is `a 1^T w` on the read token and `0` otherwise. The centered component of a non-constant edit is not determined by that identity. The hypotheses are resolved: per-token mean subtraction, residual addition, and the index of the readout. No identification is left open for the constant component. The centered component remains a full recomputation.

The identity is not a consistency score. It does not rank natural decisions. It does not see the seed-16 sign once the constant component has been removed, and it does not see the quadratic remainder along `u`. It is pre-norm LayerNorm plus addition. Retaining it as mathematics is required. Promoting it to a COR mechanism would rename the skip.

## Ordinary sensitivity, with the paths written out

The total derivative exists everywhere on this `tanh` block. It splits into the skip, the query, the key, the value, the masked softmax, the output projection, the second normalization, and the MLP. The split is the chain rule. The value-only piece is not the total. The first-order piece is not the finite response. Head derivatives add at one point. Finite leave-one-out responses need not.

These statements are useful because the earlier audits collapsed them. They are not a new decision theory.

## What does not survive as a bridge

A coordinate projection as a sufficient state, outside the vacuous constant-shift family. The structural residual as a ranking of `m`. A rank-one transport as a decision disagreement. The sentence "path derivatives sum to zero" as anything beyond `Dm=0`. Both-necessary as a synonym of interaction. The `ε=0` rank bound applied at `ε>0`.

## Why this is not a conditional bridge left open

A restricted bridge with an unresolved identification assumption would be a different verdict. The constant-shift family has no unresolved assumption. The general family has no candidate, among those written down, whose missing assumption is a credible restriction rather than the derivative itself. Leaving the general family marked `IDENTIFICATION_UNRESOLVED` in R21 records that a future score was not ruled out by a universal theorem. It does not exhibit such a score.

The examined class therefore produced correct computational facts and no distinctive relational mechanism.
