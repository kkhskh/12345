# 18. What stays open

These are scoped. None of them is a license to treat the withdrawn universal claims as open again.

1. **A coupling certificate below the product-ball maximum.** The block does constrain `Δhidden` as a function of `ΔA`. Every estimate of that function found here either reproduced the Lipschitz radius inside `B_align` or evaluated the function. A closed form that uses more of the coupling, stays in Regime 1, and is strictly below `B_align` on a nonempty open set of weights was not found and was not proved impossible. The product-ball theorem only blocks certificates that know the increments through radii alone.

2. **A sharp state-dependent majorant.** Replacing `1/√ε` by `1/s` at the base token is not a Lipschitz constant on the segment. A constant that uses the minimum of `s` along the segment would require control of that minimum. It was not derived. If it is derived later, Theorem L still forbids it from being an isotropic constant below `||∇m||`.

3. **Multi-block stacks, post-norm, RMSNorm, and attention biases inside the Jacobian.** Not differentiated. Theorem S uses pre-norm, `ε>0`, and this softmax. The final-LayerNorm section is one outer normalization, not a second block.

4. **Whether trained readouts have small `||W_O w||` or small `||W2.T w||`.** Checkable in Regime 1 on a frozen model, without fitting `R`. Not checked here. A positive finding would be an ordinary gain calculation. It would not by itself identify a relational mechanism or authorize Phase 2.

5. **Exotic finite zeros of the branch at generic weights.** Left open by the overnight search. Still open. An isolated zero defined by the branch output would be a partial evaluation of `R`, not a Regime 1 bound.

The open cells do not include a revival of `D ↓ ⇒ L ↑` or of `O` as a cause. Those remain refuted by the preserved Phase 1 and Phase 1.5 counterexamples.
