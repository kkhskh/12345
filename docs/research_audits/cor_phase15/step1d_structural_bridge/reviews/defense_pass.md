# Defense pass

Same context as the rest of Step 1D. Not an external referee. The assignment of this pass is to resist discarding a restricted theorem only because it is not universal or because its proof uses known lemmas.

## The overnight sentence was false, and the witness is legitimate

The overnight audit claimed that no B1 quantity on the injective-QKV set bounds a nonconstant finite response more tightly than the Cauchy number, without using `R` or `∇m`. CE-L-scope is such a quantity: Regime 1, `σ_min = 0.789`, nonconstant `δ`, `B_dir = 0.004051 < 0.876435`, and `|R|` underneath the bound. Dismissing it because `α = 10^{-6}` confuses a universal quantifier with a request for a typical trained model. Defense holds the distinction in `08`: valid counterexample, thin regime.

## The certificate is not only a rewritten gradient

`B_dir` does not read `∇m`. It is uniform in `H`. After eight spectral norms it applies to every state and every finite edit. A JVP does not. On the orthogonal family the gap against `B_iso` is exactly `||w|| ||δ||` at every scale, including `α = 1`, where both bounds are huge and the gap is still the proved Cauchy–Schwarz slack. That identity is worth stating because the overnight text denied it.

Theorem Kernel-out is the same point at order-1 weights. The branches move by more than `0.1` and the raw readout does not move except by `w·δ`. A circuit reader who computes `W_O w` and `W2.T w` learns an exact finite prediction that the spectral-norm bound does not state sharply. The prediction is unary. Defense does not call it relational. Defense does call it a reason to keep the factorized bound rather than only the spectral one.

## Known lemmas can still correct a false impossibility

Theorem L's proof is the mean value inequality. Using that fact to forbid every directional bound was a quantifier error. Restoring the directional bound is a correction even though each lemma is old. The literature table in `14` is the right place for that admission. The correction does not need a new proper name.

## Where defense concedes

Defense does not have a Regime 1 relation `Q` with `B_joint < B_align`. The product-ball maximum argument is accepted. The step-2 cancellation witness is accepted. The final-LayerNorm failures are accepted. The Householder pair shows that equal separate bounds can hide different responses; the object that distinguishes them is the derivative, and defense does not rebrand the derivative as a structural relation between pathways.

Defense does not ask for Phase 2. A reusable Lipschitz bound and an output-kernel identity do not identify a decision mechanism, do not touch the historical attribution gaps, and do not make `O` or `D` causal.

## Agreed residue

Both passes can sign this sentence: the directional certificate is a true restricted bound that the overnight synthesis excluded; it is standard analysis; it is not a relational causal bridge; the excluded universal claim stays withdrawn.
