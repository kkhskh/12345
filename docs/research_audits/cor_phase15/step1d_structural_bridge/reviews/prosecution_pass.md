# Prosecution pass

Same context as the rest of Step 1D. Not an external referee. The assignment of this pass is to show that every proposed relation is ordinary operator theory, a Jacobian, or an approximation lemma.

## Theorem S

`B_dir` is the triangle inequality on `w·δ`, `w·ΔA`, and `w·ΔM`, with each branch increment replaced by a Lipschitz majorant. The majorants are the mean value inequality for LayerNorm, for softmax, for the bilinear score map on a bounded set, and for `tanh`. A reader who already knows those lemmas can reconstruct S without any COR vocabulary. The improvement of `B_dir` over `B_iso` is Cauchy–Schwarz slack on the skip, which is one inner product, not a path interaction. The improvement of `B_align` over `B_dir` is `||W_O w|| ≤ σ(W_O)||w||` and the same for `W2`. Prosecution conclusion: S is correct and it is not a new mechanism.

## The small-scale comparison with `||∇m|| ||δ||`

At `α = 10^{-6}` the gradient is `w` plus a perturbation of size `2.62×10^{-7}`. The Cauchy number is essentially `||w||`. `B_dir` is the branch majorant, which was made small by multiplying `W_O` and `W2` by `10^{-6}`. Comparing them shows that a small Lipschitz constant is smaller than `||w||`. That is arithmetic. It does not show that the certificate knows something the skip does not know. The skip contribution to `R` was removed by the hypothesis `w·δ = 0` before the comparison started.

## R1

`J_A + J_M` is defined to be the derivative of `α + μ`. The derivative of the sum is the sum of the derivatives. The finite response is the integral of that derivative. Calling the integral a relational mechanism restates the fundamental theorem of calculus. The step-2 witness, `|R| = 0.169` with a numerically zero linear term, shows that the base-point slogan does not survive. The Householder pair shows a finite-response difference with identical separate bounds; the distinguishing statistic is `∇m·δ`. Prosecution conclusion: there is no residual content once the object is named as the gradient.

## R2

The maximum of a sum of independent linear functionals on a product of balls is the sum of the maxima. Orthogonality of the subspaces does not couple the scalars `w·a` and `w·m`. The proof in `10` is two lines. A joint Euclidean packaging is worse, by a square gap that factors. Prosecution conclusion: R2 has no theorem beyond the separate bound.

## R3

`|R_m - R_s|` is `w·ΔA + w·ΔM` by algebra. Bounding it by `B_branch` repeats Theorem S. The language of causal abstraction is available and is not earned beyond the citation that an implementation error controls an intervention error. The opposite-margin pair shows that a zero structural residual does not select a decision. Prosecution conclusion: R3 is an approximation lemma and a warning against reading residuals as obstructions.

## On the open set

Continuity of a strict inequality produces a neighborhood. The neighborhood is a neighborhood of small `rate`. Prosecution does not dispute the topology. It disputes any reading of that neighborhood as evidence about trained Transformers. No such reading is present in `08`, and none should be added.

## What prosecution does not claim

It does not claim that Theorem S is false. The grid checks and the second forward stand. It does not claim that CE-L-scope is an invalid counterexample to the overnight universal sentence. A small-weight counterexample to a universal quantifier is valid. It claims that the true sentences are standard, and that the overnight overstatement should be withdrawn without being replaced by a new named mechanism.
