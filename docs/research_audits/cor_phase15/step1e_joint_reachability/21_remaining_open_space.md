# 21. What remains open

The following are outside every theorem in the register.

- A moving attention pattern. One nonzero query/key draw already leaves the scalar formula. No substitute certificate was proved.
- A Euclidean ball, or any family with more than one direction, for the nonlinear block. The edit `(1, 1.2)` shows that the special bound does not extend by continuity in an informal sense; a positive-radius ball around the line contains that kind of edit once the ball is large enough to leave the line.
- Generic MLP weights. The graph lemma still holds for an invertible fixed value map. The support gap does not.
- Final LayerNorm as an objective. Note 14 is only a counterexample to transfer.
- Depth greater than one block, trained weights, and any claim about a pretrained model. None were run.
- A dependence-preserving enclosure that is cheaper than maximizing the block on a general family and still strictly tighter than the exact marginal sum. That comparison was not achieved, and it was not claimed.

A negative result here would be: for a stated larger class, every certificate that uses only separate marginal ranges is already equal to the joint support, or every strict improvement requires enough of the joint map to evaluate the response. That theorem was not proved. The open set is the complement of the line, not a universal impossibility.
