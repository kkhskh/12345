# 23. Next research decision

Stay in Phase 1.5, Step 1. Do not open Step 2. Do not open Phase 2. The letters in note 20 stay as they are.

A later step would be justified only by a question this package does not answer: whether a joint-support gap strictly below the exact marginal sum can be certified for a moving-pattern block on a genuine multi-direction family, from frozen weights, without evaluating the response family and without collapsing to the joint Jacobian. If that certification costs the same optimization as the response, the finding should be recorded as a complexity statement rather than as a predictor.

The line theorem should be left as it is. Extending it by renaming the monotone graph, or by comparing it again with a product of norm balls, would repeat Step 1D's mistake in the other direction.

No empirical protocol is authorized by this result. There is no decision label, no candidate set, and no quantity that separates a correct choice from an incorrect one.
