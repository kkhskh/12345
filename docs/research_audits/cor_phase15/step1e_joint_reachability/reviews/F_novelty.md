# Reviewer F — novelty

Same-context pass, not an external referee.

Strip the block language and what remains is: two strictly monotone scalar functions of one parameter cannot attain both of their maxima at once if the slopes have opposite signs. That fact does not need a Transformer. The linear version is the norm of a sum.

The block language is a correct sufficient condition for those slopes. It is an application, and a narrow one. I do not see a theorem that would be stated in a verification paper as new. I also do not see a hidden identification method: the weights were selected to satisfy the slope condition.

The verdict `STEP1E_VERIFIED_RELATIONAL_CERTIFICATE_KNOWN_THEORY` matches this reduction. A verdict that called the gap a new causal relation would not.
