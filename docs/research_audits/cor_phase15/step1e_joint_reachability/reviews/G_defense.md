# Reviewer G — defense of the restricted result

Same-context pass, not an external referee.

The result that should not be discarded is the distinction between a graph and a support gap. The constant-fiber lemma shows that this pre-norm block, with an invertible fixed-pattern value map, really does make `b` a function of `a`. The negative-gain example shows that the function can still hit the corner. Opposite monotonicity is a further, checkable restriction, and under it the inequality is strict for every positive gain, not only for the gain whose maximum is zero.

That is a real statement about jointly reachable increments. It is restricted to a line, a fixed pattern, and a rank-one MLP. Those restrictions are the theorem, not a reason to pretend the product ball was the Transformer. They are also not a reason to report "no dependence exists." Dependence exists and, on this line, it moves the support.

The defense stops there. It does not extend the bound to a ball, a moving pattern, a final-normalized objective, or a decision.
